4 · Measurement guidance

Step drawdown: Brent's October 16 procedure calls for water levels immediately after pump start, at 2 and 5 minutes, then every 10 minutes during each one-hour step; flow readings every 15 minutes. Continue pumping between steps. After shutdown, record recovery every 5 minutes for the first 30 minutes and then every 15 minutes, up to 60 minutes or return to static.

48-hour pumping and recovery: Brent's 48-hour sheet lists close early measurements then progressively longer intervals. His November 20 response allows overnight manual groundwater readings every 4 hours on the first night and every 6 hours on the second. Recovery follows the pumping schedule, with his conditional four-hour stability provision and final reading at 48 hours. Use the original sheets and hydrogeologist direction to plan exact visit times; this form does not schedule reminders.
